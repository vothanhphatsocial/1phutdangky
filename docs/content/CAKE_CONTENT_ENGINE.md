## 5. Affiliate CTA inheritance and disclosure

The published Cake article and its configured primary offer are the starting evidence. The setup check verified source-to-page equality. Before reuse, confirm the offer and exact href remain current, and report any destination behavior that cannot be verified.

- Find the actual primary register/apply CTA used by the existing Cake 2in1 article in project source and identify whether it is affiliate.
- Preserve its `href` exactly (redirect URL, `utm_*`, `sub*`, encoding, link IDs, target/rel, analytics mapping). Never URL-decode/re-encode or “clean” tracking parameters.
- Use current CTA component/shared affiliate config where possible rather than hardcoding a copy in multiple files.
- Store exact verified URL, provenance file and date in workbook (not as a guessed link).
- If multiple outbound CTAs exist, trace the actual *rendered registration* CTA. If verification is impossible, do not substitute generic Cake landing page and do not publish link-dependent new CTA blocks until clarified.
- Place prominent CTA in acquisition/how-to pages; medium contextual CTA in qualification/comparison; low or **none** on debt payment, emergency loss/fraud, safety, disputes and errors.
- Place transparent disclosure beside affiliate CTA: 1 Phút Đăng Ký may earn a commission if an eligible visitor registers.
- Do not mistake Cake's official referral program/campaign for this website's separate affiliate tracking. No user-owned invite code is assumed.

## 6. Content gate: article vs section

A new URL needs all relevant evidence: (a) independent search intent, (b) enough standalone helpful material, (c) official source readiness, (d) low cannibalization with Cake 2in1 pillar and other Cake site articles, (e) a justified internal-link position. `Article if validated` in workbook means **not automatically approved**.

Otherwise implement H2/H3/FAQ, pricing table or answer block within the appropriate existing canonical owner, recording action `Section`. Never turn simple synonyms `mở/đăng ký/làm` into duplicate pages. Keep support answers useful without forced sales CTAs.

Content structure preference: **question → direct answer → applicable conditions/exception → steps/examples → original source/date → safe next action**. Use author/reviewer and review date patterns the repo already has. Avoid FAQ rich-result gimmicks or unsupported “AI schema”.

## 7. Financial/regulatory and safety guardrails

Never promise automatic credit approval, a universal limit, “miễn phí mọi trường hợp”, a fixed cashback, “0% cho tất cả”, guaranteed delivery time or approval speed. Distinguish debit balance vs credit spending, purchase transactions vs cash advances, timely full repayment vs minimum repayment, rate vs conversion fees and foreign transaction costs.

For payment/interest topics, explain possible credit/late-payment consequences without encouraging debt avoidance or providing individual financial advice. For cards and verification, never request or collect OTP, PIN, CVV, full credentials, passwords, photos of sensitive ID documents or biometric evidence via this website. No fake income details, eKYC bypass or cash-out loopholes. Direct security/repayment support to official Cake/VPBank channels and source them.

7. Run available lint, typecheck, tests, build, inspect desktop/mobile responsiveness and check important internal links/CTA behavior. Do not disable rules/tests to force pass.

9. Deploy through current Cloudflare workflow only when authorized/triggered by existing process. Don't create Cloudflare project, change DNS, credentials or environment variables.
10. Verify actual production URLs and CTA/canonical/meta/mobile, if accessible. Never assert push/deploy done without confirmation.
